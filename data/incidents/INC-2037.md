# INC-2037: Sensor timed out waiting for upstream in orders_etl

- **Date:** 2026-08-21
- **Severity:** SEV-2
- **Pipeline:** orders_etl

## Summary

On 2026-08-21, the wait_for_orders_export task failed in the orders_etl pipeline. Failure type: Sensor timed out waiting for upstream. The daily sales dashboard and the finance revenue report showed stale data until the fix.

- 02:38 Alert fired: wait_for_orders_export failed.

## Root cause

The upstream export job never wrote the _SUCCESS marker within the six-hour sensor timeout, usually because the export failed or ran very late.

## Resolution

Ask the export owner to fix or re-run the export. Once the marker exists, clear the sensor task.

## Follow-ups

- Alert the export owners at the expected finish time, before the sensor times out.
- Document export SLAs for every upstream source.
