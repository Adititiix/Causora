# INC-2038: Kafka consumer offset out of range in clickstream_ingest

- **Date:** 2026-08-25
- **Severity:** SEV-3
- **Pipeline:** clickstream_ingest
- **Failed task:** consume_clickstream
- **Duration:** 138 minutes
- **Impact:** the web funnel dashboard and product analytics

## Summary

On 2026-08-25, the consume_clickstream task failed in the clickstream_ingest pipeline. Failure type: Kafka consumer offset out of range. The web funnel dashboard and product analytics showed stale data until the fix.

## Timeline (UTC)

- 02:36 Alert fired: consume_clickstream failed.
- 02:46 On-call engineer acknowledged and opened the task log.
- 03:38 Root cause identified.
- 04:26 Fix applied and the failed task cleared.
- 04:54 Pipeline completed and dashboards refreshed.

## Root cause

The consumer's committed offset pointed to data the broker had already deleted, because the consumer group was stopped for longer than the broker keeps data.

## Resolution

Reset the consumer group offsets to the earliest available. Clear the failed task.

## Follow-ups

- Alert on consumer lag.
- Do not pause the consumer group for long maintenance windows.
